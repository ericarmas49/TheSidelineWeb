1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your browser, device, or computer when you visit a website or use an application.

Cookies help websites and applications recognize devices, remember user preferences, improve functionality, analyze usage, and support security and operational activities.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies, including:

  • pixels;
  • web beacons;
  • software development kits ("SDKs");
  • local storage technologies;
  • device identifiers; and
  • similar tracking technologies.

For convenience, this Cookie Notice refers to all such technologies collectively as "Cookies."

  • personalizing content and user experiences;
  • supporting community, messaging, profile, and account features;
  • preventing fraud and enhancing security;

  • supporting analytics and reporting activities; and
  • developing and improving products, services, features, and technologies.
